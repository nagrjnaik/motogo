Location Access Required

iOS / Browser blocked the GPS prompt

To broadcast your live motorcycle position to your pack, your browser needs permission to read your GPS. On iPhone (iOS), the prompt is suppressed if Location Services is turned off in any of these 3 places:

1 Opened inside WhatsApp? Open in Safari

WhatsApp's built-in preview browser on iPhone often blocks GPS prompts. Tap the Safari compass icon (🧭) at the bottom-right corner of your screen to open this link in real Safari.

2 iPhone System Settings (Most Common on iOS)

Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Safari Websites, select While Using the App, and switch on Precise Location.

3 Safari Address Bar Website Settings

Tap the aA or Page Settings icon on the left side of the Safari address bar → tap Website Settings → Location → Allow, then refresh the page.

About motoGo.

Zero-Rupee Pack Radar for Riders

What This App Does
📡
Live Multi-Bike GPS & Distance Tethers
Tracks every rider on a shared map with live Haversine distance badges, compass bearings, and total pack spread.
💬
1-Tap WhatsApp Room Invites
Generates a fresh random Session ID (`MG-XXXX`) every ride. Forward the link on WhatsApp—no login walls or app installs.
🛑
Stop Signals with "Copy That" Confirmations
Drop a fuel, chai, or SOS stop marker at your coordinates and see live checkmarks as your pack taps Accept.
🧭
Instant Google Maps Handoff
Tap any separated friend's pin or stop signal to launch turn-by-turn Google Maps navigation straight to their live spot.
